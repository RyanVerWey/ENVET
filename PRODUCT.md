# Product

## Register

brand

## Evidence

Strategic context is drawn from `.plan/PROJECT.md`, the M2 and M3 brainstorms, and `docs/facebook-source-audit.md`, supplemented by the user's instruction to execute the milestones and include a blog. This records the agreed mission and existing planning constraints, not owner acceptance of a finished design.

## Users

United States veterans and their families seeking a human introduction to ENVET, plus donors, volunteers, and community partners. Visitors may be privacy-conscious, unfamiliar with horses, or using a phone in a distracting environment.

## Product Purpose

Make equine therapy options at Eagle's Nest Veterans' Equine Therapy understandable and approachable. Help visitors contact the team and help donors reach the verified donation destination. First release needs no database. Owner reviews locally before any production URL is connected.

## Brand Personality

Human, grounded, respectful. Calm confidence and real farm photography, as specified in M3. Honor service without defining people by injury, diagnoses, or hardship.

## Anti-references

The M2/M3 no-gos exclude patriotic kitsch, military stereotypes, corporate healthcare styling, guilt-driven fundraising, medical promises, invented testimonials, decorative animation, and generic repeated card grids.

## Design Principles

- A human conversation is the first step, not a sensitive intake form.
- Authentic evidence comes before claims; owner decides publication readiness.
- Give Get Support and Donate distinct, direct paths.
- Publish practical, source-grounded answers, not search-engine filler.
- Keep the first release operationally simple and accessible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA target from the project plan. Keyboard and touch support, visible focus, reduced motion, semantic landmarks, readable body copy, descriptive links, and CorvaUI mint light/dark themes. No autoplay or pressure timers.
