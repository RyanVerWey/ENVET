## How can I support ENVET?

**You can give through ENVET’s official PayPal donation page, share the organization’s work, or contact the team about current volunteer and in-kind needs.** A useful contribution starts with what the program needs now and what you can comfortably offer.

ENVET provides equine therapy options for United States veterans and their families in Lovettsville, Virginia. Its official page describes free access to rescued or donated horses for veterans. Community support helps the program keep going.

## Make a donation through the official channel

Our [donation page](/donate) links to the PayPal destination shared by ENVET’s official Facebook page. Choose your gift and complete it on PayPal. Check the recipient details before making a payment, and keep your confirmation.

The website does not collect your payment-card details. If you need organizational documentation or want to discuss a different way of giving, [contact ENVET](/contact).

We do not assign a made-up outcome to a donation amount. Ask the team about current priorities if you want to understand what support is needed most.

## Share with someone who might be looking

A veteran or family member may not know ENVET exists. Sharing the [official Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/profile.php?id=100068209587248) or the [visit information](/visit) can help them find a place to ask questions.

Keep the invitation gentle. You do not need to assume someone wants services or ask them to disclose personal circumstances. A simple “I came across this and thought you might want to know about it” leaves the choice with them.

## Ask before offering supplies or a delivery

In-kind gifts are easiest to coordinate when they match a current need. Before buying supplies, arranging equipment transport, or offering a horse, speak with the team about suitability, capacity, and delivery arrangements.

Something generous can still create extra work if there is no place to store it or no current use for it. Confirmation helps both the donor and the organization.

## Explore volunteering without assumptions

Ask about current roles, requirements, scheduling, and any orientation that may be needed. Don’t assume that an interest in helping means you should arrive at the farm or approach the horses without an arrangement.

You can describe the time or skills you would like to offer. The team can tell you whether there is a suitable opportunity now.

## Start with one useful action

Support does not have to be complicated. [Give if you can](/donate), share the mission thoughtfully, or [ask what would help](/contact). Each is a way to show up for the people and horses at Eagle’s Nest.
