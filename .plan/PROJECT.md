# Project: ENVET

Created: 2026-09-06T02:13:54Z

## Vision

Create a trustworthy digital front gate for Eagle's Nest Veterans' Equine Therapy. Veterans and families should understand available support and reach a human quickly. Donors and community partners should see authentic, approved evidence of the work and support it through verified channels.

## Principles

- Owner approval precedes public launch.
- Use authentic ENVET facts and permission-cleared media.
- Protect veterans, families, children, and identifiable visitors.
- Keep first release database-free and operationally simple.
- Build with CorvaUI mint light/dark and WCAG 2.2 AA intent.

## Constraints

- No production URL until owner accepts private review build.
- Facebook supplies source material; production site must not depend on Facebook rendering or CDN URLs.
- No sensitive document upload or medical-data collection in initial release.
- Database work begins only after funding and workflow approval.

## Planning Rules

- Specs are the canonical execution contract.
- Stories are created only after spec approval.
- Stories should be execution-ready and verification-aware.
