# Brainstorm: M2 Messaging and experience architecture

Started: 2026-09-06T02:22:59Z

## Focus Question

Turn approved facts into trauma-informed site structure and conversion paths for veterans, families, donors, volunteers, and partners.
## Desired Outcome

Approved sitemap, page contracts, messaging hierarchy, and action paths for veterans, families, donors, volunteers, and partners.

## Vision

Every visitor knows within seconds whether ENVET is for them and whether to request help, donate, volunteer, or learn more.

## Supporting Material

- .plan/brainstorms/m1-source-of-truth-and-media-rights.md
- https://www.facebook.com/profile.php?id=100068209587248

## Constraints

- Trauma-informed, plain language.
- No invented outcomes, credentials, or services.
- No sensitive-document upload or database workflow.

## Open Questions

- Final eligibility language and owner-approved service taxonomy.
- Preferred contact path for veterans and families.

## Ideas

- Define sitemap, page goals, content model, and calls to action.
- Keep intake human and low-risk before database funding.
## Raw Notes

## Refinement

### Problem

Raw Facebook information does not provide a coherent, accessible website journey or clear conversion hierarchy.

### User / Value

Veterans reach a human safely; donors understand need and impact; owner controls claims.

### Appetite

Content and UX planning only.

### Remaining Open Questions

Service names, family eligibility, appointment model, volunteer opportunities, and approved impact evidence.

### Candidate Approaches

Mission-first multi-page structure with repeated contextual actions and a simple human contact path.

### Decision Snapshot

Prioritize Get Support and Donate. Support secondary Volunteer, Facebook, WhatsApp, phone, email, and Messenger routes.

## Challenge

### Rabbit Holes

Blog, resource library, complex intake wizard, and SEO content volume before core pages are approved.

### No-Gos

No coercive fundraising, medical promises, military stereotypes, or proof-document collection.

### Assumptions

Visitors may be stressed, skeptical, privacy-conscious, or unfamiliar with equine services.

### Likely Overengineering

Personalized journeys and content management before content stabilizes.

### Simpler Alternative

Five to seven focused pages with clear calls to action and FAQ coverage.

## Specs

- Define priority audiences and visitor jobs
- Approve sitemap and page-level content contracts
- Write trauma-informed services and eligibility messaging
- Map donation, volunteer, and contact conversion paths
- Define content governance and claim approval rules
