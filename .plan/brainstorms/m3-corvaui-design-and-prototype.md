# Brainstorm: M3 CorvaUI design and prototype

Started: 2026-09-06T02:22:59Z

## Focus Question

Create owner-reviewable mint light and mint dark visual direction using CorvaUI and approved ENVET photography.
## Desired Outcome

Owner-approved high-fidelity design direction, responsive templates, and accessibility contract using CorvaUI mint light and mint dark.

## Vision

Authentic farm imagery, calm confidence, and unmistakable action paths make ENVET feel human and credible without looking clinical or templated.

## Supporting Material

- https://github.com/RyanVerWey/CorvaUI
- .plan/brainstorms/m1-source-of-truth-and-media-rights.md
- .plan/brainstorms/m2-messaging-and-experience-architecture.md

## Constraints

- CorvaUI mint light and mint dark required.
- WCAG 2.2 AA target, reduced motion, keyboard and touch support.
- Use only approved media shortlist.

## Open Questions

- Final logo assets, preferred visual anchors, and owner choice among design probes.

## Ideas

- Define design tokens, responsive layouts, components, accessibility, and motion.
- Produce prototype only after owner-approved shape brief.
## Raw Notes

## Refinement

### Problem

Site needs distinctive visual trust while serving users with varied abilities, devices, and emotional contexts.

### User / Value

Owner can judge real direction before build; implementers get stable tokens and component behavior.

### Appetite

High-fidelity responsive prototype and design specification, not production code.

### Remaining Open Questions

Photography crop rules, theme default, logo quality, and motion intensity.

### Candidate Approaches

Image-led farm narrative with restrained mint interface surfaces and a deliberately useful dark mode.

### Decision Snapshot

Use CorvaUI as system base. Complete Impeccable shape, visual probes, explicit owner selection, then prototype core routes and states.

## Challenge

### Rabbit Holes

Custom design-system fork, decorative animation, novelty interactions, and generic card grids.

### No-Gos

No patriotic kitsch, corporate healthcare aesthetic, guilt imagery, inaccessible overlays, or unapproved photography.

### Assumptions

Authentic horses, farm scenes, and community activity provide strongest visual trust signals.

### Likely Overengineering

Building every component before page direction is approved.

### Simpler Alternative

Prototype homepage, services, donate, and contact patterns plus shared header/footer and key states.

## Specs

- Establish CorvaUI mint light and dark theme contract
- Create and approve visual direction probes
- Design responsive core page templates and components
- Specify accessibility, interaction, and motion behavior
- Define photography art direction and alt-text standards
